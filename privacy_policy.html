AI Art Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Art Studio ("the app", "we", "us"), developed by KartikRoc, collects, uses and protects your information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a unique user ID through Google Firebase Authentication. This is used only to create and manage your account.
  • Text prompts: The text you type to create an image is sent to Pollinations.ai, a third-party AI image generation service, so the image can be generated. We do not use your prompts for any other purpose.
  • Generated images: Images you create are saved locally on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers.
  • App usage data: A simple count of images generated is stored on your device only.

2. How we use your information

  • To let you sign in and use the app
  • To generate images from your prompts
  • To show your saved images and statistics
  • To respond to your support requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Third-party services

The app uses these third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Pollinations.ai (image generation) – pollinations.ai
  • Google Play Services – policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data security

All data sent between the app and these services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

5. AI-generated content

Images are created by artificial intelligence and may sometimes be inaccurate or unexpected. You can report any offensive or inappropriate image using the Report option in the app. You must not use the app to create illegal, harmful, sexual or hateful content.

6. Children's privacy

AI Art Studio is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Delete your account and data

You can delete your account and all related data at any time:

Inside the app: Open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. Your account is removed from Firebase and all images and data are deleted from your device immediately.

Without the app: Send an email to kartikgupta618@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my AI Art Studio account" from the Google account you used to sign in. We will delete your account and associated data within 7 days and confirm by email.

No data is kept after deletion.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
